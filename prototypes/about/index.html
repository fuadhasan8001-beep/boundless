BORN IN THE NIGHT LAB.
CODED BEYOND THE SYLLABUS.

We are an autonomous syndicate of 5 student engineers, creative technologists, and visual hackers based at University Lab Building C, Room 402. Rejecting the homogenizing sludge of corporate software, we build tactile hardware, custom shader stacks, and sensory architectures.

[§] ORIGIN_DOC ////////////////////////////////

Academic lectures were too slow. The industry was too sterile.

In late 2023, while classroom tracks assigned boilerplate CRUD apps and hypothetical marketing decks, five undergrads smuggled heavy-duty soldering gear, modular synthesizers, and GPU rigs into the underutilized backroom of Building C.

We wanted physical kinetic resonance, tactile interfaces that glow when stroked with high-frequency RF signals, and real-time GLSL rendering engines running unthrottled at 120 FPS. Room 402 became an unsanctioned digital forge.
