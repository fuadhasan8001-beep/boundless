COMMISSION PROTOCOLS & FAQ

Strict technical transparency is our core thesis. Before submitting a dispatch, verify our operating principles and delivery constraints.

[01] DO YOU TAKE COMMERCIAL CLIENT PROYEK? +
Yes, under specific criteria: the work must challenge existing spatial or web technologies and yield novel sumber terbuka components. We avoid corporate generic CMS rollouts and boilerplate enterprise portals. If you need bespoke WebGL pipelines, physical projection interactions, or wild interactive experiments, we will assemble a student-engineer sprint team.
[02] ARE ALL LAB EXPERIMENTS OPEN-SOURCE? +
90% of our codebase is published under MIT or GPLv3 on our GitHub organization. For commissioned work with confidential spatial or festival IPs, we retain the right to abstract and publish the underlying toolchains, custom shaders, and driver bridges.
[03] CAN STUDENTS FROM OTHER CAMPUSES JOIN THE COLLECTIVE? +
Yes. While our physical research rig is situated on the Tech Campus in Surabaya, our core software sprint contributors work asynchronously across global nodes. Submit your GitHub or portfolio through this terminal or ping `#hackers` on Discord.
[04] WHAT TECH STACK RUNS IN THE LAB? +
We specialize in WebGPU, Three.js, WGSL shaders, Rust/Wasm engines, TouchDesigner, ESP32 microcontrollers, OpenCV computer vision, and bespoke real-time audio synthesis pipelines.
